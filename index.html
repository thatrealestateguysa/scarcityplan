The "Empty Fry Box" Playbook

Strategic real estate systems for the modern agent.
WIN listings. PRICE correctly. CREATE urgency.

The Core Principle

Buyers don’t respond to how much stock is actually in the box – they respond to how much feels like is left. Your listings work the same way.

  • Too empty: feels desperate.
  • Overflowing: no urgency.
  • Almost not enough: healthy pressure, stronger offers.

“We design price, access and information so serious buyers feel: ‘If I wait, I might lose this.’”

Micro-Dominance

Own a few streets, not the whole city. Specialists win.

Accuracy

Price inside the correct search brackets, not at random numbers.

Velocity

Short, intense campaigns beat slow, noisy ones every time.

The Formula

Correct Price + Controlled Access + Phased Marketing = Perceived Scarcity & Stronger Offers

The Core Playbook

Four modules to run the Empty Fry Box system.

1. Market Analysis 2. Positioning 3. Pricing 4. Campaigns

Module 1: Reading the Market

Hot Seller Market

Signs: Low stock, quick sales.

Move: Manage traffic and set deadlines.

Balanced Market

Signs: Average DOM, value sensitive.

Move: Be Top 3 value and Top 3 photos.

Cool Buyer Market

Signs: High stock, price cuts.

Move: Either be the bargain or the rare find.

Module 2: Own Your Micro-Areas

The Selection Matrix

Select 1–3 lanes to dominate:

[Suburb] + [Type] + [Price]

Weak: “I sell everywhere.”

Strong: “I specialise in full-title homes in Lonehill between R2.5m and R3.5m.”

Positioning Script

When asked “Why you?”:

“I’m the specialist for this pocket. I track the real numbers, price you to beat your competition and run structured campaigns that create demand instead of desperation.”

Module 3: Pricing for Scarcity

Search Bracket Rule

Buyers search in logical brackets (R2–R2.5m etc.).

Wrong: R2,050,000 – invisible in most searches.

Right: R1,995,000 – more eyeballs, looks like value.

Top 3 Value Test

“If I were a serious buyer with cash, would this home be in my Top 3 to view this weekend?”

Yes: launch hard.

No: fix price or condition first.

The 14–21 Day Rule

Days 1–7: Launch

Max exposure. Track enquiries vs viewings.

→
Days 8–14: Feedback

No second viewings or offers = market rejection.

→
Days 15–21: Adjustment

Don’t drift. Adjust strategy before the listing goes stale.

Module 4: Campaign Structure

Phase 1: Pre-Launch

Prime your buyer list. Offer early viewing slots.

→
Phase 2: Launch

High-impact photos, strict viewing windows, clear call-to-action.

→
Phase 3: Adjust

If strong, set deadlines. If weak, adjust by day 21.

Dynamic Tactics

Select the current temperature of your market to see the right moves.

Hot Market Tactics

Goal: Harness demand without underselling.

Access: Tight viewing windows, group buyers together.

Photos: High impact, not too many – create curiosity.

Scripts

To Seller:

“If we sharpen the price, your home becomes the scarce opportunity buyers fight for. We control traffic so they see each other and act quickly.”

To Buyer:

“Correctly priced homes in this pocket are not staying on. If this is on your shortlist, I recommend we book you in now.”

Balanced Market Tactics

Goal: Become the curated, obvious choice.

Access: Flexible but intentional – “Shall I reserve a slot for you?”

Scripts

To Team:

“We don’t run open buffets. We run curated experiences. When we respect the listing, the market respects the price.”

Cool Market Tactics

Goal: Create relative scarcity in an oversupplied market.

Positioning: Lead with the one feature that makes this listing rare.

Scripts

Price Adjustment:

“The market has spoken. We can either chase it down slowly, or adjust once and become the must-see home of the month.”

Lead Generation

Use scarcity to start high-value conversations with owners.

Limited Slot Price Check

Turn your research into a scarce service instead of a cold-call pitch.

“I’m opening four slots this week for a ‘Market of the Moment’ price check for owners in your pocket. I show you what’s actually sold and what is sitting. Reply PRICE if you’d like me to hold a slot for you.”

Buyer Match Message

“I’m working with three qualified buyers looking for a home like yours in this pocket. Before we talk about putting a board up, I can first check if your home is a match for any of them.”

Implementation

Run a 7-day sprint with a simple weekly review.

The 7-Day Fix

Day 1: Audit

Label each mandate as Hot, Balanced or Cool. Spot the stale fries.

Day 2: Redesign

Update hero photo, headline and description for clarity and value.

Day 3: Alignment

Call the seller with the numbers and agree on the 21-day plan.

Day 4: Relaunch

Refresh portals, send to your buyer list and reset the clock.
