The Empty Fry Box Principle

Fast-food brands learned that demand doesn’t come from how many fries are in the box – it comes from how many fries it looks like are left.

  • Too empty: feels desperate.
  • Overflowing: zero urgency.
  • Almost not enough: healthy pressure, stronger offers.

The FOMO Machine uses that same psychology with suburb stats so your launch feels like “last chance” – not “we’ve been sitting”.

PLAY THE MARKET YOU’RE IN

Dynamic Tactics for each market type.

Once the FOMO Machine has called your market, plug into the matching playbook below.

Seller’s Market

Goal: protect price while harnessing demand.

Access: one or two tight viewing windows so buyers feel the crowd.

Photos: 8–12 hero-level images that prove value but don’t tell the full story.

Conversation lines:

To seller: “If we.launch just below the noisy band of stock, your home becomes the scarce opportunity buyers fight for – not one more price-reduced listing.”

To buyer: “Correctly priced homes in this pocket aren’t staying. If this one is on your shortlist, we should get you in early.”

Balanced Market

Goal: become the obvious “Top 3” choice.

Access: controlled flexibility – “Would you like me to reserve a slot for you?”

Presentation: flawless photos and copy. Average homes sit; the curated ones move.

Conversation line:

To team: “We don’t run open buffets. We run curated experiences. When we treat every viewing as an event, buyers treat the price seriously.”

Buyer’s Market

Goal: create relative scarcity in an oversupplied world.

Positioning: lead with one rare feature – stand size, view, complex position, security or levies.

Price: you’re either the best-value option or the rare find. Nothing in between cuts through.

Conversation line:

Price adjustment: “The market has spoken. We can chase it down slowly, or make one decisive move now and position your home as the must-see opportunity this month.”

KEEP LISTINGS OUT OF “STALE FRY” TERRITORY

A simple 7-day sprint and weekly review.

The 7-Day Fix

  1. Day 1 – Audit: run the FOMO Machine for every mandate. Label each as seller’s, balanced or buyer’s market.
  2. Day 2 – Redesign: update hero photos, headline and description to match the market type.
  3. Day 3 – Alignment: call the seller with the numbers and agree on the 21-day plan.
  4. Day 4 – Relaunch: refresh portals, send to your buyer list and.track enquiry vs viewing again.
  5. Day 5 – Traffic check: compare enquiries, online views and showings against your market norms. Log objections.
  6. Day 6 – Adjust: if response is light, make one decisive move on price, access or creative based on feedback – not a series of small, weak tweaks.
  7. Day 7 – Report: send the seller a simple 7-day “what we learned” wrap and confirm the next 7-day action plan.
